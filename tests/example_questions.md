# Example Retrieval Questions

These questions were used to manually test retrieval quality.

## CV Test Document

1. What AI experience does Ali have?
2. What programming languages are listed?
3. What projects are included?
4. What education background is shown?
5. What experience does Ali have with Git-based workflows?

## Expected Behaviour

The app should retrieve source chunks from the relevant CV sections rather than unrelated parts of the document.
